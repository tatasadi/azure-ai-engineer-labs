ACME CORP — EMPLOYEE TRAVEL & EXPENSES POLICY
Document version: 2.4 — Effective 2026-01-01
Owner: Finance Department (finance@acme.example)

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines what business travel and work-related expenses ACME Corp
employees may claim, and the limits that apply to each category. All figures are
in euros (EUR) unless otherwise stated. Claims that exceed these limits require
written pre-approval from a line manager.

2. GROUND TRANSPORT
2.1 Taxi and ride-hailing rides are reimbursed up to a maximum of EUR 50 per ride.
    Rides above EUR 50 must be pre-approved and accompanied by a written
    justification.
2.2 Public transport (bus, tram, metro, train 2nd class) is reimbursed in full
    with a valid receipt.
2.3 Personal car mileage is reimbursed at EUR 0.30 per kilometer.
2.4 Airport parking is reimbursed up to EUR 25 per day.

3. MEALS
3.1 Daily meal allowance (per diem) is capped at EUR 60 per full travel day.
3.2 A single business dinner with a client is reimbursed up to EUR 90 per person.
3.3 Alcoholic beverages are NOT reimbursable under any circumstances.

4. ACCOMMODATION
4.1 Hotel stays are reimbursed up to EUR 180 per night within Europe.
4.2 Hotel stays outside Europe are reimbursed up to EUR 240 per night.
4.3 In-room minibar and pay-per-view charges are not reimbursable.

5. FLIGHTS
5.1 Economy class is the default for all flights under 6 hours.
5.2 Premium economy is permitted for flights longer than 6 hours.
5.3 Business class requires director-level approval regardless of duration.

6. EQUIPMENT & INCIDENTALS
6.1 Work-related SIM/data while abroad is reimbursed up to EUR 15 per day.
6.2 Printing and stationery are reimbursed up to EUR 20 per trip.

7. SUBMISSION RULES
7.1 Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.
7.2 Every claim above EUR 25 requires an itemized receipt.
7.3 Reimbursement is paid within 14 business days of an approved claim.

END OF POLICY
